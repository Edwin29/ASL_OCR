CN7-28 / PA0 = UP
CN7-30 / PA1 = DOWN

CN7-34 / PB0 = RIGHT

PC0 = PAGE PREVIOUS
PC1 = CONFIRM (release sends SHORT or LONG)
PC2 = MODE LEVER (LOW=capture, HIGH=reading)
